# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences with equal weight, both deciding whether to hand a build to Casas Eco and then calling on WhatsApp:

- People who want to build a home or second residence in the region (Garopaba, Praia do Rosa, Imbituba, Encantada, Campo Duna).
- Investors building chalets, lofts or a pousada to rent out in the summer season.

Most arrive on a phone, often from an Instagram or WhatsApp link.

## Product Purpose

Presentation portfolio for Casas Eco, a builder and building-materials store in Garopaba/SC. It shows real built work (photos, videos, area and timeline) so the visitor trusts the company and starts a conversation on WhatsApp. Success is a WhatsApp chat started.

## Positioning

Builder and building-materials store in the same company: one integrated service from project to finish, better purchase conditions, less rework. Own headquarters and structure in Garopaba for more than 20 years. Partnership with CasaBio for ecological sewage treatment.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare builders by looking at finished houses and how fast they were delivered. The showroom is at SC-434, km 3, Campo Duna, Garopaba/SC, open Monday to Friday 8h to 18h and Saturday 8h to 12h. Contact goes through WhatsApp (48) 99675-4334.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step; can be hosted on GitHub Pages or as a claude.ai artifact.
- Building systems offered: wood (autoclave-treated pinus; single wall, double wall with PET wool, double wall with drywall), masonry, and mixed wood + masonry. Also decks and pergolas.
- Videos must stay light enough to open on a phone connection.
- All copy in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Casas Eco. Logo at `img/logo.png` (dark green mark).
- Tagline in use: "Casas especiais em madeira e alvenaria." / "Sua casa, seu estilo, seu futuro. A natureza faz parte."
- Instagram @casasecomadeiras, site casaseco.com.br.

## Evidence on Hand

- Numbers: more than 150 builds in the region; more than 20 years in the market.
- Projects with real data: Chalé A-frame (Praia do Rosa, 60,33 m², 30 days); Pousada com vista pra lagoa (Praia do Rosa, 210 m², two lofts in 90 days, full pousada in 150 days); Casa 2 pisos (164 m², 50 m² external, 3 bedrooms, 120 days); Chalé 76 m² (36 m² deck and veranda, 90 days); Casa 2 pisos com vista pra lagoa (150 m², double wall with drywall).
- Photos in `img/` (hero, store, aerial, systems, 14 gallery photos, CasaBio); videos with posters in `video/` (three roof-building clips, one per project, and Rafael explaining builder + materials).
- Two real Google reviews: Silvana Thormann (Garopaba) and Eduardo Frigeri (Imbituba), both mentioning Sr. Guilherme.
- No prices, no client list beyond those two reviews: do not invent either.

## Product Principles

1. Real work is the proof: every claim points at a built house, a number or a video.
2. WhatsApp is always one tap away.
3. Phone first: fast, light, legible outdoors.
4. Speak to both the family and the investor without splitting the site in two.
